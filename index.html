Frequently asked questions

A few helpful things to know before you begin.

What is Safe Space Therapy?

It is a gentle wellness space to check in with your feelings, explore affirmations, and reflect through journaling.

Are affirmations a replacement for therapy?

No. Affirmations and journaling can support reflection, but they are not a substitute for professional mental health care.

Are my journal entries saved?

Yes. Your journal entries and saved affirmations are kept in your account, so you can read them on any device you sign in to. Your therapist only sees the entries you choose to share, plus a short weekly summary.

How do I enquire about a session?

Go to Services & Booking and send the enquiry form. The therapist will reply by email. Sending the form does not confirm an appointment yet.
