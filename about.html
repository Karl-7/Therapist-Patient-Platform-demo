A LITTLE ABOUT US

A space where you can be yourself

Safe Space Therapy is a gentle place to check in with your feelings, explore supportive affirmations, and make time for reflection.

I am a psychologist and certified behaviour analyst who blends the science of mental health with a deep respect for people’s stories. My aim is to offer a steady, non-judgemental space where you can understand yourself and move forward at your own pace.

My path into this work began with a BA in English (Honours), followed by two master’s degrees at the University of Edinburgh: Psychology of Mental Health and English Literature. I hold ABAT certification and am a CDA-licensed Behaviour Analyst in Dubai, UAE.

My approach

My work is collaborative, evidence-informed and tailored to the person in front of me. Sessions are not about fixing you; they are about helping you understand your patterns, build practical tools and feel more at home in your own life.
